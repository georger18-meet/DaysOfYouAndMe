In your unity project, scroll down the Project assets area and find the Packages

Right click - show in explorer

Enter the Packages folder on your computer

Copy-Paste this zipped file into the Packages folder

Extract it

Delete the zip file

After unity loads the new package, go to the Settings folder, and in your Renderer file (mobile or pc or whatever) - click on it, see in the inspector Add Render Feature button

click the Add Render Feature button and add the Light Scattering Render Feature

Now choose your RP Asset (mobile or pc or whatever) and click V for Depth Texture

Now go to your Global Volume, add an Override, and add this:

OccaSoftware --> Light Scatteing

This only works if you have a directional light in the scene

Add Fog Density, change its color to differ from the Distant Fog in Unity's Light Settings and remember it works alongside your Directional Light's angle
